Status: draft, awaiting owner answers

## Problem
Managing an apartment renovation means tracking many tasks, suppliers, quotes,
payments and deadlines, usually spread across WhatsApp, spreadsheets and memory.
The same needs exist, at larger scale, in an architecture office.

## Goal
One shared, phone-friendly place where the owner and family see what is being
done, by whom, when, and how much it costs, built so it can grow into an
architecture office ERP.

## Users (phase 1)
- Owner: plans, approves, pays.
- Family/partner: follows progress, adds notes and photos, suggests changes.

## Scope ideas (to confirm)
- Rooms/areas and the work planned for each
- Tasks and schedule (status, dates, responsible)
- Suppliers and quotes (compare, approve)
- Budget vs. spent (payments, receipts)
- Photos and documents (before/after, plans)

## Out of scope for phase 1
Clients, proposals, timesheets, invoicing (future ERP phases).
